Welcome to the EI Lab

The Empathic Interface Lab (EI Lab) investigates human-centered immersive technologies by integrating Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Empathic Computing. Our research focuses on understanding human perception, cognition, and emotion through multimodal sensing, including eye tracking, physiological signals, brain activity, and human behavior analysis. We develop adaptive immersive systems for education, training, collaboration, and interactive experiences.
