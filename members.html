Immersive Empathic Interface Lab

Dr. Sungchul Jung
Assistant Professor, Lab Supervisor

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Game Design and Development at Kennesaw State University. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida in 2018. I was a postdoctoral fellow researcher at the Human Interface Technology Lab (HIT Lab) in New Zealand, from 2018 to 2021. My research interests lie in the field of eXtended Reality (VR/MR/AR) and data-driven Empathic Computing based Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) perspective. Based on the findings from my research, I am leveraging the fully personalized virtual reality experience for social relationships, education, learning and collaboration, entertainment, simulation, game design, and healthcare. I am actively working on academic service as a reviewer, program committee, and editor at prestigious venues such as IEEE VR, ACM VRST, IEEE ISMAR, ACM CHI, and Frontiers.

Nicholas Wile
Graduate Research Assistant, M.S. Computer Science Student

I'm an M.S. Computer Science student and Graduate Research Assistant at KSU. I graduated summa cum laude from KSU in 2022 with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and a Minor in Computer Game Design and Development. I've been working in the IEI Lab since January 2023, designing, developing, and extending virtual reality applications, primarily for education, fitness, and medical simulations. I also integrate peripheral devices like eye trackers and wearable sensors with VR to collect real-time player data for VR UX research. In 2023, I built an eye-tracking VR Classroom and conducted user studies with 24 students on campus, leading to 1st place recognition in the KSU Computing Showcase for an innovative prototype enabling VR UX research, studying Avatar-Mediated Interactions in simulated learning environments. My current project involves validating the accuracy of a facial emotion recognition machine learning model using animated virtual avatars. My areas of interest include game design and computer graphics, with additional experience in AI and computer vision for games and XR.
