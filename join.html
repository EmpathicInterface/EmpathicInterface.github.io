Join the EI Lab

We welcome motivated students interested in human-centered AI, extended reality, empathic computing, and human-computer interaction.

Who We Welcome

The EI Lab welcomes Ph.D., M.S., and undergraduate researchers who are interested in solving challenging research problems through interdisciplinary collaboration.

Required

  • Technical background in Computer Science, Computer Game Design and Development, or a related discipline.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with other team members.
  • Knowledge or experience in at least one of the following areas: Unity 3D, Unreal Engine, NVIDIA Omniverse, Virtual Reality, or Augmented Reality.

Recommended

  • Proficiency in C++, C#, Python, or a similar programming language.
  • Knowledge or experience in Human-Computer Interaction, Cognitive Science, Empathic Computing, Computer Graphics, Computer Vision, or User Studies.

How to Apply

Students interested in joining the EI Lab should submit a current resume using their KSU student email account.
